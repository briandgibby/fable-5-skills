## Keep In Main Harness If

- The behavior is broadly useful across most nontrivial tasks.
- The instruction is compact.
- It improves routing, context recovery, output choice, or verification.

## Split Into Separate Skill If

- It needs sustained domain knowledge.
- It needs many references, templates, scripts, or examples.
- It has distinct activation triggers.
- It would slow down ordinary tasks.
- It carries special trust, safety, permission, or source-use requirements.
