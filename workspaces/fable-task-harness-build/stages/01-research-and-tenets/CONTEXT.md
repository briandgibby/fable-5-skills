## Process

1. Decide whether the task needs external research or local synthesis only.
2. Prefer primary and near-primary sources.
3. Update the source ledger with links, claim summaries, and project implications.
4. Update the wiki synthesis with adopted, rejected, and deferred tenets.
5. Keep source content summarized in original words.
